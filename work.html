Case Studies

Selected work

Three HR systems I built and owned end to end — combining Workday HRIS depth with hands-on Power Platform automation. Each turned a manual, error-prone process into a reliable, self-running one.

Workday · HR Analytics

Workday Reporting Suite

Workday Report Writer Dashboards Power BI HR Metrics
Problem

HR leaders needed timely, trustworthy people data — headcount, turnover, lifecycle movements — but reporting was manual, slow to compile, and prone to inconsistency between sources. Decisions were being made on numbers no one fully trusted.

What I built

As a Workday Certified Reporting Specialist, I owned the full reporting layer: a suite of custom Report Writer reports plus three dashboards — Management, Business Unit, and HR Insight — covering core metrics like headcount and turnover. I automated the recurring monthly reporting cycle so the numbers refreshed reliably instead of being rebuilt by hand each month.

Result

Accurate, on-time HR reporting HR leaders could act on, with materially improved reporting accuracy and the manual monthly effort largely removed — freeing time for analysis instead of assembly.

HR Platform · Power Apps

Employee of the Quarter Platform

Power Apps Power Automate SharePoint Governance & Audit
Problem

Recognition ran on scattered emails and spreadsheets — hard to run fairly, impossible to audit, and easy to lose track of. There was no transparent, defensible way to move from nomination to a final, announced winner.

What I built

An end-to-end recognition platform on the Power Platform covering the full flow: nomination → voting → results dashboard → announcement. I built in governance and an audit trail so every step was fair, transparent, and reviewable — the difference between a fun poll and a program HR can stand behind.

Result

A repeatable, self-running recognition program with a clean audit trail, replacing manual coordination and giving the business a transparent, trusted selection process.

Automation · Power Platform

Outsourcing Management App

Power Apps Power Automate SharePoint Lists Approval Workflows
Problem

Managing outsourced staff — leave, absence, and resignation — was a fully manual process spread across emails and files. Tracking was fragile, approvals were slow, and records were hard to reconstruct when they mattered most.

What I built

I re-engineered the whole thing into one centralized Power Platform solution: SharePoint Lists as the system of record, a Power Apps front end for submitting and managing leave, absence, and resignation cases, and Power Automate flows driving approvals and notifications. One place to track every request, route it for sign-off, and keep a clean record.

Result

A single source of truth for outsourced-staff workflows — faster, routed approvals, reliable record-keeping, and far less manual chasing across the leave, absence, and resignation lifecycle.
