Part 01Why I build

It started inside the system.

I didn't come to automation from engineering. I came to it from HR governance — working inside our core HR system every day, and noticing how many processes still sat outside it, running on email and manual effort.

The core system did its job.

Hiring, promotions, transfers, contract changes — the lifecycle ran through it, and I was inside it every day, configuring and reporting. That's where the interest started.

But some processes still sat outside it.

In HR governance I kept finding steps the system never touched — chased over email, remembered by a person, collected by hand. Every one of them could be automated. That's what made me curious.

So I designed the flow and built the app.

Power Automate for the routing and reminders, Power Apps for the part people touch, SharePoint as the record. The manual step designed out — triggered on time, visible, auditable.
