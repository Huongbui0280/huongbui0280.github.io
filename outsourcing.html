The problem

There was no app for this

Full-time staff had HR systems. Outsourced staff had nothing built for them. Every leave day, maternity plan, unplanned absence and resignation was handled by hand — an email here, a spreadsheet there, a message someone forgot to log. No single record, no routing, no way to answer "who approved this?" when an audit asked.

How it works

One flow, three moving parts

Data goes in through one door, routes to the right approver, and every step is written to a record that can't be quietly changed.

01 Data input

One structured form

Staff submit a single form — leave, maternity, absence or resignation — with the exact fields HR needs. No free-text emails, no missing dates. The data is clean the moment it's captured.

02 Security & routing

The right eyes only

Each submission routes automatically to the right manager. Staff see only their own requests; approvers see their team. Permissions are enforced by the system, not by trust.

03 Records & audit

Answers in one click

Every action — submitted, approved, rejected — is timestamped and attributed. When an audit asks "who approved this, and when?", the answer is right there.
